PARTNERSHIP DEED

This Deed of Partnership is made at Surat on 1st April 2022 between:
1. Shri Rakesh Bhai Patel, aged 45, residing at Adajan, Surat (First Partner); and
2. Shri Mehul Bhai Desai, aged 39, residing at Vesu, Surat (Second Partner).

The partners have agreed to carry on the business of manufacturing and trading of textiles in partnership under the name and style of M/s. Patel Desai Textiles, with its principal place of business at Ring Road, Surat.

1. The capital of the firm shall be Rs. 50,00,000/- contributed equally.
2. The profits and losses shall be shared equally.
3. Each partner shall be entitled to a monthly remuneration of Rs. 60,000/- as permitted under Section 40(b) of the Income Tax Act.
4. Interest at 12 percent per annum shall be paid on capital.
5. The bank accounts shall be operated jointly.
6. The partnership is at will. Any partner may retire by giving three months' notice.
7. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
