# Hyperframes Composition Brief: LawWeb (technical cut, v3)

## Objective
A narrated ~96 s technical walkthrough of LawWeb for YC founders, balancing architecture and measured results, with real captures of the app answering on this laptop. v1 (`brag-output-2026-10-14-151742/`) and v2 (`brag-output-2026-10-14-170958/`) are kept unchanged.

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output-2026-10-19-053936/composition/`
- Video: `brag-output-2026-10-19-053936/brag.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, 96.2 s, -16 LUFS); poster `brag.jpg` (hook frame, baked as frame 0)

## Source material
- Every on-screen number comes from the "Verified numbers" table in `brag-plan.md`.
- Live clips: `assets/live/live-en.mp4` (capture `en4`) and `live-hi.mp4` (capture `hi2`), recorded 2026-10-19 from the real app (main's server code, the PR #66 client) with the judgment, precedent and classifier indices loaded. Capture used headless Chrome at 2x via a screenshot loop, with nvidia-smi sampled every 250 ms on the same clock.
- Each clip has one edit: the search wait is compressed (7x English, 16x Hindi) under a constant "waits compressed Nx" badge, beside a timer showing real seconds since submit. Typing and the answer play at 1x.
- The English scene's summary line gives the measured split: search + grading, then the model's first token.
- The 1.0 s figure in the model scene and the outro is labelled as the LLM call alone (prompt replay) and paired with the live search time.
- Capture and clip tooling: `scripts/capture/` (`capture2.mjs`, `build_clip.py`, which exports the stage split, `warmup.py`, `link_runtime.sh <worktree> [index dir]`). Scenes with live footage are generated by `scripts/gen_live_scenes.py` from `assets/live/*.json`.

## Creative direction
Polished; a research paper typeset in LawWeb's print look; one figure per scene with its test set named; fade-through-paper transitions. No name credit and no "academic project" wording (user rule). No PR numbers on screen.

## Scenes
1. Hook: live VRAM reading.
2. Live capture + nvidia-smi.
3. System architecture: GPU / CPU / indices.
4. LangGraph corrective loop.
5. Hybrid retrieval: hit@3 0.897 on 39 questions.
6. Multilingual pipeline + live Hindi answer.
7. IL-TUR.
8. Trained classifiers.
9. Document pipeline latency.
10. Model selection: LLM-call figures, prompt replay.
11. Hardware budget.
12. Results + repo link and email.

## Audio
- Narration: Kokoro `af_heart` at 1.1x, one clip per sentence (`scripts/gen_vo.py`), grouped as `voiceover`. Lines s2a and s10b were re-recorded for v3.
- Music: vol-12 bed, re-carved against the voiceover group (strength 0.8) after retiming, with fade lanes.
- SFX: three quiet effects; the send click is re-synced to the new clip at 8.8 s.
- Finish: `scripts/finish.py` (two-pass loudnorm, poster bake).
