LawWeb: Indian legal answers cited to the exact bare-act section, from a 4B model running entirely on a 4 GB laptop GPU. A LangGraph agent with hybrid retrieval over 15,100 statute sections and 1M+ Supreme Court passages, a statute classifier at 38.35 macro-F1 on IL-TUR (published LeSICiN baseline: 28.08), and Hindi plus 15 other Indian languages, every number measured. Code: github.com/anubhab7111/lawWeb
