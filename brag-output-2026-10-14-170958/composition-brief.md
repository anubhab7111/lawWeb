# Hyperframes Composition Brief: LawWeb (technical cut, v2)

## Objective
A narrated ~96 s technical walkthrough of LawWeb for YC founders, balancing architecture and measured results, with a real capture of the app answering on this laptop. v1 (`brag-output-2026-10-14-151742/`) is kept unchanged.

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output-2026-10-14-170958/composition/`
- Video: `brag-output-2026-10-14-170958/brag.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, 96.2 s, -16 LUFS); poster `brag.jpg` (hook frame, baked as frame 0)

## Source material
- Every on-screen number comes from the "Verified numbers" table in `brag-plan.md` (re-run evals, committed results, PR bodies).
- Live clips: `assets/live/live-en.mp4` and `live-hi.mp4`, captured from the real app (FastAPI + Ollama + React) in headless Chrome at 2x by screenshot loop, with nvidia-smi sampled every 250 ms on the same clock. The English clip ran with `qwen3:4b-instruct` (PR #64, unmerged); the Hindi clip also includes the rendering fix in PR #65 (unmerged). Waits are sped up (8x / 16x / 4x, badged on screen) and streaming plays in real time; the elapsed timer shows real seconds since submit.
- Capture and clip tooling: `scripts/capture/` (`capture2.mjs`, `build_clip.py`, `warmup.py`, `link_runtime.sh`); scenes with live footage are generated by `scripts/gen_live_scenes.py` from `assets/live/*.json`.

## Creative direction
Polished; a research paper typeset in LawWeb's print look; one figure per scene with its test set named; fade-through-paper transitions; no name credit and no "academic project" wording (user rule).

## Scenes
1 hook (live VRAM reading) · 2 live capture + nvidia-smi · 3 system architecture (GPU / CPU / indices) · 4 LangGraph corrective loop · 5 hybrid retrieval (hit@3 0.897, 39 questions) · 6 multilingual pipeline + live Hindi answer · 7 IL-TUR · 8 trained classifiers · 9 document pipeline latency · 10 model selection (answer-call figures, prompt replay) · 11 hardware budget · 12 results + repo link and email.

## Audio
Kokoro `af_heart` at 1.1x, one clip per sentence (`scripts/gen_vo.py`), grouped as `voiceover`; vol-12 music bed carved against that group (strength 0.8) with fade lanes; three quiet SFX. Finish with `scripts/finish.py` (two-pass loudnorm, poster bake).
